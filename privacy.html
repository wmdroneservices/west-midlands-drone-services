Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

West Midlands Drone Services ("we", "us", "our") is based in Halesowen, West Midlands. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are the data controller for the information described below.

1. Information we collect

When you submit a Request a Quote form, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number
  • The property or project address, town/city and postcode
  • Details of the service you require and your project description
  • Your preferred date and time
  • Any photographs you choose to upload to help us understand the job

We do not collect payment details through this website, and we do not use cookies to track visitors for advertising purposes.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Plan and carry out any drone flight you book with us, including airspace and site-specific checks
  • Keep a record of bookings and correspondence for our own business administration
  • Meet any legal or regulatory obligations, for example records required by our CAA operator authorisation

Our lawful basis for processing this information is that it is necessary to respond to your enquiry and, where you go ahead, to perform a contract with you.

3. How your enquiry is submitted

The Request a Quote form on this website is handled by a third-party form service, which delivers your submission to our business email address. That service processes the data solely to deliver your message to us and does not use it for any other purpose. We do not store form submissions in a separate database on this website.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with third parties, except where necessary to deliver your enquiry to us (see section 3 above), or where we are required to by law.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry, provide the service, and meet any accounting, insurance or regulatory record-keeping requirements. If you would like us to delete information we hold about you, contact us and we will do so unless we are required to keep it for a legal reason.

6. Photographs and video we capture

Aerial photography and video we take while carrying out a booking may show your property, premises, guests or other individuals present at the time of the flight. How this imagery is used, stored and shared is agreed with you separately as part of your booking, and is not covered by this website privacy policy.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have inaccurate information corrected, to ask us to delete your information, and to object to how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@scenesource.co.uk or 07522 611172.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
